## Required final source-to-result checks

1. Actual supplied raw bytes hash is checked before source selection. Payload CIK,
   history identity, explicit request identity and aware as_of/observation bind;
   naive or earlier cutoffs refuse. No live source transport or API key exists.
2. Only literal us-gaap:NetIncomeLoss/USD and Assets/USD are read. Two explicit
   income intervals, three original closing balances and two same-accession
   comparative balances resolve to seven retained groups. No longest-duration,
   latest-row, quarter substitution, fallback or inferred annual label.
3. Candidate selection uses exact accession and physical period first. A matching
   row with contradictory form/FY/focus/filed metadata refuses rather than being
   silently removed. Asset rows require absent start, never explicit null.
4. All required groups exist, agreeing duplicates are retained canonically, full
   original-present keys/absence/null distinctions survive, and exact numeric
   agreement precedes float projection. Bool/string/nonfinite inputs and
   nonpositive/unrepresentable Assets refuse. Comparative-to-original closing
   equality is exact before projection.
5. Request geometry enforces ordered intervals, consecutive fiscal labels and
   physical continuity. Beginning asset dates are exactly one day before income
   starts. History last-three labels/assets and last-two NI scalar projections
   bind to independently resolved source groups. Annual.period_end and every
   original history field remain unchanged; complete history hash includes the
   original citation/source graph and unrelated legacy fields.
6. Explicit evidence is detached/immutable, strict enough to reject malformed
   nested or unchecked copies on reconstruction, and bound to actual supplied
   history before scorer shortcuts. A supplied hash/evidence model alone is not
   advertised as source authentication; byte linkage belongs to the evaluator.
7. Actual scorer ratios use beginning denominators for roa_t, roa_p and latest
   CFO/assets. Raw/finite/zero-underflow/exact predicate checks use those actual
   operands. No final boolean override or score subtraction occurs. GP/safety
   and the five other component formulas retain their default behavior.
8. Optional evidence propagates coherently through piotroski_f_score,
   quality_score and compute_fundamental_record. Quality and Record retain method,
   source/history identity and operands; evidence absent is omitted with exact
   legacy serialized shape. Record binds available issuer/symbol/latest FY.
   It does not claim to reconstruct absent full history from a hash.
9. Any nonNone price with present evidence refuses before scoring/valuation.
   Direct present-evidence Record with value/combined refuses. No-price evaluator
   lacks a price option. Legacy priced calls and whole-winner merge behavior stay
   exact, including a legacy incoming winner dropping known evidence.
10. Outer result retains unchanged complete history, observation/assertions,
    evidence, actual old/new FScore/Quality/Record and exact ratio comparison.
    Actual known result must be default8/trendTrue -> opt-in7/trendFalse with the
    other eight verdicts unchanged. Default quality0.6675842309900554; new quality

11. Baseline snapshots/claims precede implementation; old test hashes and all
    protected source/cohort/generated bytes match. Focused actual collected RED
    precedes implementation, including actual source comparison and both synthetic
    reversal directions, constant assets and Fraction financial properties.
12. Final prose retains conditional NetIncomeLoss numerator, unrelated period,
    CFO mapping, turnover/share/split-basis, source authenticity and PIT limits.
    This is one known-defect production evaluation, not a new economic trial,
