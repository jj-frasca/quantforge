## Why approve

The slice advances a demonstrated actual-source quality-scoring defect using existing retained facts and real scorer/quality/record propagation. It neither substitutes synthetic returns for evidence nor replaces the expired source study. Existing F324 already fixes the known result and conditional numerator caveat; default behavior remains frozen. There is a legitimate bounded deliverable: the actual scorer computes beginning-scaled ROA values using source-bound positive denominators, then the actual quality blend and no-price record carry both the resulting number and method/operand/source/history identity. Post-hoc boolean overrides are expressly excluded.

Two income groups plus five Assets groups at three physical instants establish both original closing and same-filing comparative beginning values. Exact agreement is required before projection; no latest-row, desired-value or alternative-tag selection follows conflict. History is bound by its full canonical hash, actual last-three asset/last-two income scalars and fiscal labels. Explicit interval assertions are not misrepresented as original DEI audits. A model carrying a source SHA alone is expressly not authentication; the outer evaluator must verify actual bytes and row linkage.

The corrected independence from AnnualFundamentals.period_end is necessary and sound: production derives that field from revenue, while the known FY2020 revenue is a quarter and its Assets2020 is a closing instant. Requiring equality would reject the fixed witness for an unrelated legacy field and tempt a history repair. The supplied history and its original2025 envelope remain unchanged, while the new ROA evidence uses independently resolved NI/Assets periods. Unrelated component periods remain explicitly unverified.

The source observation/cutoff prevents a2026capture from answering a2022PIT query. The known8->7 comparison is disclosed before tests, including its already-known ratios and quality baseline. It supplies no alpha, market trial, price, financed account or gate measurement. No historical selection count is reset. A corrected score point alone cannot improve the alpha case.
